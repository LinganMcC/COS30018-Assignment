Generated by `python evaluation/error_analysis.py`. Selected pipeline: repaired segmentation (contours, then touching digits cut; `src/segmentation_split.py`), `otsu_mnist_box` preprocessing, VGG-deep (`cnn_best`); extension: `ext_best`.

Every failure is put in one category by comparing the boxes with the true digits: **merged** (fewer boxes than digits, no digit-sized ink thrown away – digits cut together), **dropped** (fewer boxes, and the discarded ink adds up to a digit), **split** (more boxes than digits) – all three segmentation – or **misread** (right number of boxes, a digit wrong) – recognition.
